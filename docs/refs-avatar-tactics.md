# Refs for squad tactics and avatars

Public principles only. Do not copy maps or Nintendo parts.

## Squad / cinematic third-person

Load `squad-tactics`, `plan-queue`, `overwatch-fire`, `enemy-fire`, `set-piece-action`, `adventure-tool`.
Stack `stealth-info`, `enemy-perception`, `cover-shooter`, `cutscene-handoff`.

Inner/outer vision rings, pause-command, scarce guns, and camera handoff after a climb are the reusable rules.

## Avatar / face

Load `avatar-create` (part-kit | slider-morph | hotspot) and `face-morph`.
Stack `sprite-skin`, `character-rig`, `art-bible`.

Open formats to study: [bloke](https://github.com/plopgrizzly/bloke), [UMA](https://github.com/umasteeringgroup/UMA).
Do not ship `FFLResHigh.dat` or official Mii meshes.
