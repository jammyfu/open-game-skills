# Season track

Ask first: free track, paid track, or both?

Rules: stacks under `game-monetization` battle-pass. Track items follow `iap-offers`. Catch-up may sell time, not a new cancel window. Season end does not wipe `settings-persist` or cloud saves. Required verbs stay on the free track unless the game is premium-upfront.

Accept: a player can name what is free this season. Turning the pass off still leaves a legal slice (`first-session-value`).
