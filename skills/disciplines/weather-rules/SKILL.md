# Weather rules

Ask: cosmetic | traction | stealth-noise | chemistry | none.
Rain may wet surfaces or cut traction. It may not change hitboxes.
Particles are a performance slider. The rule stays when particles thin.

Accept: the player can use weather as a tool or ignore it and still finish the hook.
