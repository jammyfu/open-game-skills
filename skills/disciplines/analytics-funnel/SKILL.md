# Analytics funnel

Ask: death-heat | quit-beat | first-session.
Events sit on the logic tick: boot, first verb, first death, first reward, quit. Do not harvest inputs that identify a person. A heat map does not replace `gameplay-validation`.

Accept: you can point at the beat that loses the most first-session players.
