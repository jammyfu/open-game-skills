# FOV comfort

Ask: fixed | player-fov | comfort-pack.
FOV is a setting (`settings-persist`). It does not change hitboxes or lock range.
Shake (`juice-vfx` / `juice-hit`) has a reduced or off flag (`a11y-controls`). Boost-rail look-ahead may not slam FOV as a hidden speed buff.

Accept: a player can raise FOV or cut shake and still finish the slice. Boxes stay the same.
