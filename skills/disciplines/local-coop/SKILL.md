# Local co-op

Ask first: shared camera or split? How many pawns? Shared inventory?

Columns: shared-cam | split-2 | split-4 | take-turns.

Rules: one simulation clock. Extra cameras drop shadows first (`performance-budget`). Shared-cam must keep both pawns on-screen. Input maps are per device (`kb-mouse-map`).

Accept: player 2 joins mid-session without a re-tutorial of verbs.
