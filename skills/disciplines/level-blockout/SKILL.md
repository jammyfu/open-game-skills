# Level blockout

Ask the column.

| Column | What you block |
|---|---|
| beat-strip | show → safe-try → test as three grey rooms |
| arena-ring | combat space with two exits and one cover idea |
| corridor-gate | a door that needs a published verb |
| vantage | player sees the next beat before they enter it |

Scale with the actor capsule. Art comes after a stranger can walk the strip.

## Iron rules

- One new verb per strip. Remix is a later strip (level-teach).
- Cover and sightlines are collision hulls, not decals.
- Do not dress a room that has no named beat.
- Moving-platform and hazard-volume must work in greybox.
- Jump height and door width live on loco / jump rows, not mesh names.

## Accept

A top-down of the greybox can be labeled show / try / test. Final meshes do not change jump distances or cone-sight. A designer can play the strip with unlit cubes.
