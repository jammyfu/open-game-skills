# Set-piece action

Ask first: rail-aim | climb-camera | full-control.

A set piece is a room with a published fail and exit, not a cut that plays itself.

| Column | Player still does |
|---|---|
| rail-aim | aim and shoot on a moving path |
| climb-camera | climb / whip / mantle; camera may go third-person for the verb |
| full-control | cover + fire in an authored arena (`cover-shooter`) |

Rules: start and end on a marked pose (`cutscene-handoff`). Finish the verb in the new camera, then hand back. Enemies may not snipe from off-camera (`enemy-fire`). Do not copy a franchise train or cargo plane.

Accept: after the piece, look and fire work on the same actor clock.
