# Death carry

Ask: checkpoint-full | drop-bag | run-reset | none.
Wake point is save-checkpoint or rest-site. Unlocks do not flip.
Drop-bag is a world pickup with a lifetime. Run-reset is a roguelike column, not an action-game surprise.
Death music does not stack.

Accept: a player can say what they lost and where they woke. A death does not mark a boss beaten.
