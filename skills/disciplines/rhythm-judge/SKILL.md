# Rhythm judge

Ask first: song-clock, action-clock, or both?

Rules: judge windows are frames or ms on one clock. Action-feel hitstop may pause notes only if the column is action-rhythm. Off-window is a miss, not a secret buffer. Accessibility may widen windows (`a11y-controls`) without changing the chart.

Accept: a tap on the published center is Perfect. Training can show early/late.
