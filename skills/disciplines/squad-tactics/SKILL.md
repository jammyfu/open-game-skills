# Squad tactics

Ask: role-kit | ghost-clear | loud-fail.

A squad is 3–6 bodies. Each body has one job and one rare verb (`enemy-kit-balance` one-job rule, applied to allies). Shared verbs (carry, distract, silent take) exist so no body is a 10-second ending cameo.
View is top-down or iso so cones and patrols are readable (`stealth-info`, `enemy-perception`). Guns are last resort — a shot is a published noise (`projectile-hitscan`) that wakes a ring.
Do not copy a mission layout. Do not give one body every verb.

Accept: a tester can name each body's unique verb and a room that needs two bodies at once.
