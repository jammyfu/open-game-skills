# Super meter

Ask first: cancel-spend, invuln-reversal, or cinematic-super?

Rules: gain and cost are tables. Spending is a node on `combo-design`. Invuln frames are published (`dodge-iframe` cousin). Meter does not change hitstop. Burst that freezes the world is a cutscene, labeled.

Accept: a player can say how they gain and what one bar buys. Empty meter still leaves a legal poke.
