# Hazard volume

Ask first: tick-damage, instant-kill, or push-field?

Rules: authored boxes, not the pretty mesh (`hitbox-hurtbox`). Tick uses the same clock. Instant-kill needs a tell (`attack-tell`) or a published pit (`fall-rules`). Hazards do not steal i-frames unless listed. Region tier may add a volume, not rewrite feel.

Accept: a stranger can point at the safe tile. Standing on the art lip is not a secret death.
