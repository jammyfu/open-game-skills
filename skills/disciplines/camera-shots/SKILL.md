# Camera shots

Ask: side-plane | orbit-third | first-person | rail | lock-follow.
Fighter-plane games use side-plane. Open field uses orbit-third. Boost-rail uses look-ahead.
Do not change shot mid-hitstop. Decollision stays on during a slam.
One shot column per mode. Pause menus may cut; combat may not.

Accept: a player can play 30 seconds mute and still know where they face.
