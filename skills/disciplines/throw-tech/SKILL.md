# Throw tech

Ask first: break-window, command-grab, or both?

Rules: throw is a volume or range (`hitbox-hurtbox`). Break is frames on the same clock. A grab that cannot miss is a bug. Throws ignore armor unless published (`hyper-armor`).

Accept: you can whiff a throw on purpose. Training draws grab range.
