# Ghost line

Ask: off | own-best | downloaded | race-pack.
A ghost is recorded inputs replayed on the same track (`racing-feel`). It does not rubber-band.
If the ghost clips geometry after a patch, drop it; do not warp.
Replay files are data. They are not live netcode.

Accept: racing a ghost never changes your own max speed.
