# Stealth info

Ask: cone-alert | suspicion-meter | full-sim-sound.
Detection is a cone + light + noise. Alert has a tell before Combat (`enemy-ai`).
Failing stealth drops into an encounter, or a reset the player can see. Instant game-over is a column, default off.
HUD may show the cone the guard owns. It may not paint the official path.

Accept: a player can name why they were seen (stood in light, ran, opened a loud door).
