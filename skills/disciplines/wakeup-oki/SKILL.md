# Wakeup oki

Ask first: quick-rise, delayed-rise, or both?

Rules: knockdown follows hitstun (`hitstun-recover`). Rise invuln is a published window. Meaties lose to at least one rise option. Infinite knockdown is a combo-graph bug.

Accept: stand / roll / attack are nameable. Training draws the rise window.
